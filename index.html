Frequently Asked Questions

Find quick answers about appointments, emergency care, reports, and hospital services.

How can I book an appointment at Lumina Health Care?

You can book an appointment online through our website, call our appointment desk, or visit our hospital reception directly. Our team will confirm your schedule as soon as possible.

Is emergency service available 24/7?

Yes. Our emergency and trauma care unit is open 24 hours a day, 7 days a week, with trained doctors, nurses, ambulance support, and critical care facilities.

Can I choose a specific doctor for consultation?

Yes. You may select your preferred doctor while booking an appointment. Doctor availability may vary depending on consultation hours and patient schedules.

Do you provide diagnostic tests and lab reports?

Yes. Lumina Health Care offers modern diagnostic services, laboratory tests, imaging support, and digital report delivery for patient convenience.

Do I need to bring previous medical reports?

Yes, if available. Previous prescriptions, test reports, and medical documents help our doctors understand your health history and provide better treatment.

Where is Lumina Health Care located?

Lumina Health Care is located in Uttara, Dhaka, Bangladesh. You can find the full address and contact information in the Contact section of this website.
